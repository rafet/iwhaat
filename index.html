IWHAAT, for short.

You asked me something I don't know. If I tried to answer anyway, I'd open an AI chat, paste in your question, and read you back whatever it said.

That makes me a meat proxy, and a slow one. You'd be waiting on me. I'd be missing the context that's in your head. And the answer would reach you filtered through someone who understands the question no better than you do.

So skip me. Ask it yourself.

Then there's the part it can't do: reading the whole thing rather than the first confident-sounding line, holding it against what you already know, trying it if it's the kind of thing you can try.

If you're still stuck after that, come back. "I asked, it said X, but Y doesn't add up" is a question I can actually help with. That's where a human is worth something.

This isn't a brush-off. It's the fastest way to your answer.
